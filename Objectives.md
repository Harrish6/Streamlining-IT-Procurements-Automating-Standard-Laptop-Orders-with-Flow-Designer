# Objectives

1. To automate the processing of standard laptop requests.
2. To reduce repetitive manual activities in IT procurement.
3. To create a consistent workflow for standard laptop orders.
4. To automate approval and fulfilment steps where applicable.
5. To improve visibility of request progress and status.
6. To reduce the possibility of manual processing errors.
7. To gain practical experience with ServiceNow Flow Designer.
8. To demonstrate the use of workflow automation in an IT service environment.
